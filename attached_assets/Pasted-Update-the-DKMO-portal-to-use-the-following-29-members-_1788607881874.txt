### Role-based permissions

Do not give every committee member unlimited admin access.

Their permissions should be **role-based**. For example:

* President — highest committee-level oversight.
* Vice President — committee oversight and delegated authority.
* General Secretary — meetings, membership administration, committee records and correspondence.
* Treasurer — payments, receipts and financial records.
* Loan Convenors — loan-related functions.
* FRF Convenors — FRF-related functions.
* Auditor — audit/financial verification access.
* Verification Team Leader — verification functions.
* Event Organizers — event functions.
* Employment Scheme members — employment-scheme functions.
* Executive Members / Advisors — committee participation and the permitted committee functions.
