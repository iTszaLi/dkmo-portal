# IMPORTANT STRUCTURE CHANGE

There is NO separate FRF Membership module anymore.

The system now operates as:

### DKMO Membership

Every approved DKMO member automatically becomes eligible for:

* Family Relief Fund (FRF)
* Medical Aid
* General Relief Fund
* Loan Scheme
* Emergency Response Support
* Job Bureau
* Air Ticket Assistance
* India Representative Support

Membership and FRF eligibility are now unified.
