10. Membership Fee and FRF Contributions Must Never Be Mixed







This is a critical requirement.







The Membership Fee and FRF Contributions are two completely separate financial systems and must never be merged, combined, or calculated together.







Membership Fee Rules



One-time registration fee.



Fixed amount: SAR 100.



Paid only once when becoming a member.



Status:



Paid



Pending



Unpaid



Has no relationship to FRF claims.







Example:







Member joins DKMO → Pays SAR 100 Membership Fee → Membership becomes active.







FRF Contribution Rules







FRF is not a membership fee.







FRF contributions are created only when there is an approved FRF case such as:







Death Benefit



Emergency Assistance



Air Ticket Support



Other approved FRF assistance







For every approved FRF claim:







Every active member receives a contribution obligation.



Default contribution amount = SAR 50 per claim.



This amount should be configurable by admin.



Contributions are tracked separately for each claim.







Example:







Claim FRF-001 Approved







100 Active Members







Expected Collection:







100 × SAR 50 = SAR 5,000







Each member receives a separate FRF contribution record for FRF-001.







Later another claim is approved:







FRF-002 Approved







Again each active member receives a new SAR 50 obligation.







Therefore a member may have:







Claim	Amount	Status



FRF-001	SAR 50	Paid



FRF-002	SAR 50	Pending



FRF-003	SAR 50	Paid







The system must support this structure.







Financial Separation







Never combine:







Membership Fee Due



Membership Fee Paid



FRF Contributions Due



FRF Contributions Paid







They must have separate:







Tables



Calculations



Reports



Statistics



Dashboard widgets

Payment records

11. FRF Eligibility Rules







This is a mandatory business rule.







A person is NOT eligible for the FRF Scheme immediately after submitting an application.







A person becomes eligible for FRF benefits and FRF contribution obligations only after:







Membership application is approved.



SAR 100 Membership Fee is fully paid.



Member status becomes Active.







Only then should the member be enrolled in the FRF Scheme.







FRF Enrollment Logic



Applicant Stage







Applicant has submitted a membership application.







Status:







Not a Member



Not FRF Eligible



Cannot receive FRF benefits



Cannot be charged FRF contributions



Approved But Membership Fee Not Paid







Application approved but SAR 100 not paid.







Status:







Membership Pending Activation



Not FRF Eligible



Cannot receive FRF benefits



Cannot be charged FRF contributions



Active Member







SAR 100 Membership Fee paid successfully.







Status:







Active Member



FRF Eligible



Can receive FRF benefits (subject to rules)



Can be assigned FRF contributions







At this moment the system should automatically enroll the member into the FRF Scheme.







FRF Claim Collection Rules







When an FRF claim is approved:







Generate SAR 50 contribution records only for:







✅ Active Members







Do NOT generate FRF obligations for:







❌ Applicants







❌ Rejected Applicants







❌ Members with unpaid SAR 100 membership fee







❌ Inactive Members







❌ Suspended Members







❌ Archived Members







FRF Benefit Eligibility







A person can only become an FRF claimant or beneficiary if they were already an FRF-eligible active member.







Example:







Ahmed applies today.







Application approved today.







Membership Fee SAR 100 not paid.







After one week a Death Benefit claim occurs.







Result:







❌ Ahmed is not eligible because he never became an active FRF member.







Example:







Ahmed pays SAR 100.







Status becomes Active.







System enrolls him into FRF Scheme.







Later an FRF event occurs.







Result:







✅ Ahmed is eligible according to FRF rules.
