# DKMO Community Services & Welfare Expansion

In addition to the Membership, Referral, and FRF systems, I would like the portal to represent the complete scope of DKMO activities as shown in our official brochures and organizational objectives.

The portal should not appear to be only a membership database. It should function as a complete DKMO management platform.

---

## Create a New Main Module

### DKMO Community Services

This module will serve as the central welfare and community support hub.

Services include:

* FRF (Family Relief Fund)
* Medical Aid
* General Relief Fund
* Emergency Response Assistance
* Loan Scheme
* Air Ticket Assistance
* Job Bureau
* India Representative Support
* Community Welfare Activities

---

Build all modules using reusable architecture so new welfare programs can be added later without major redesign.

The DKMO Portal should become a complete Membership, Welfare, FRF, Community Services, and Committee Management platform rather than only a membership administration system.
