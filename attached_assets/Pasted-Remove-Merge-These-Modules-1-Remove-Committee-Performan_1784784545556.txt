Remove / Merge These Modules 🟡
1. Remove: Committee Performance ❌

Current:

Committee Performance

Members Recruited
Fees Collected
FRF Referred
Loans Processed
Score
Leaderboard

My opinion: remove it.

Why?

It creates unnecessary competition inside a welfare organization.
Scores can become controversial.
Many committee members will have roles that cannot be measured equally.

Example:

A Treasurer collecting money gets points.

An Event Organizer arranging a huge event may get 0.

Not fair.

Replace with:
Committee Activity Report

Only show:

Tasks completed
Meetings attended
Responsibilities handled

No ranking.
