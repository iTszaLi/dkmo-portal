One feature I would definitely add

FRF Completion Certificate

When an FRF case closes, automatically generate a summary showing:

* Beneficiary Name
* Target Amount
* Total Collected
* Number of Contributors
* Date Opened
* Date Closed
* Collection Percentage
* Distribution Status

This creates a permanent, printable record for transparency and future audits.

Overall, I would aim for a portal that feels like professional membership software rather than just a member list. The key principles are automation, transparency, auditability, and a clean modern interface. Those four things make a huge difference in day-to-day use.
