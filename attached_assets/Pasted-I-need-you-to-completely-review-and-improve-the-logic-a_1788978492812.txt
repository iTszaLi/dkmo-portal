# 6. MEMBER ELIGIBILITY

This is extremely important.

Only members whose **membership fee is PAID** should be eligible to contribute to an FRF case.

The logic must be:

### Membership Paid

→ FRF Eligible

### Membership Unpaid / Pending / Not Paid

→ FRF Not Eligible

Therefore:

If a member has not paid the one-time SAR 100 membership fee:

* They should NOT receive an FRF SAR 50 obligation.
* They should NOT appear as payable in the FRF Fees case.
* They should show as "Not FRF Eligible" if displayed in the member database.

If membership later becomes Paid, the member can become eligible according to the current active FRF case rules.

Do NOT confuse the one-time SAR 100 membership fee with the SAR 50 FRF contribution.

They are completely separate.
