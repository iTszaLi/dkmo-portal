FRF Referral & Ambassador System Restructure

After reviewing the current FRF Membership and FRF Claims modules, I would like to restructure the referral and recognition system to ensure there is a single source of truth for recruiter performance and award eligibility.

Core Requirement

All referral rankings, recruiter recognition, ambassador performance tracking, and Momento Award eligibility must be based only on Approved FRF Membership referrals.

Under no circumstances should FRF Claims data influence recruiter rankings or award eligibility.

Rankings MUST use:
Approved FRF Membership referrals
Approved FRF Membership applications
Referral points generated from approved memberships
Membership recruitment performance
Rankings MUST NOT use:
FRF Claims submitted
FRF Claims approved
FRF Claim amounts
Claimant activity
Claim processing statistics
Any claim-related metrics

Example:

✅ Member recruits 25 people who become Approved FRF Members → Counts toward rankings and award eligibility.

❌ Member submits, processes, or benefits from 100 FRF Claims → Does NOT affect rankings or awards.

FRF Claims Module Cleanup

The FRF Claims module should remain focused exclusively on claim management.

Keep claim-related fields such as:

Claimant
Beneficiary
Claim Type
Claim Amount
Claim Status

However, remove any referral, recruiter, ambassador, award, ranking, or membership recruitment logic from the FRF Claims module.

All referral tracking must exist only under:

FRF Membership → Referral Tracking → FRF Ambassador Leaderboard

Final Objective

Create a single, automated FRF referral recognition system where recruiter rankings, ambassador status, Momento Award eligibility, award history, reports, and dashboard leaderboards are generated entirely from Approved FRF Membership referrals, while FRF Claims remain completely separate and have no impact on rankings or awards.
