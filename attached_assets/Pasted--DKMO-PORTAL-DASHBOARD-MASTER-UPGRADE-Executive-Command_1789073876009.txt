# 22. FRF BUSINESS RULES MUST BE RESPECTED

The authoritative DKMO rule is:

Membership fee = SAR 100 one-time.

FRF contribution = SAR 50 per member per FRF case.

A member must have an eligible/paid membership before their FRF contribution becomes active.

If membership is unpaid:

* FRF must be inactive/not eligible
* They must not be counted as a collectible FRF contribution

FRF collection is sequential:

* One active collection case at a time
* Approving a claim opens the next collection case
* A new case cannot become active while the previous case is still active
* When no collection case exists, dashboard must show no active collection case
* Previous cases remain historical
* Payments must remain associated with their original FRF case

Do not change these rules while redesigning the dashboard.
